# Realistic use cases / 现实使用案例

These are realistic test prompts, not claims about private user outcomes. They show when the skill should activate and what a useful response must produce.

以下是现实化测试问题，用来说明何时应调用本 Skill，以及一个合格回答应当产生什么结果；它们不代表虚构的用户成功案例。

## 1. Career change / 转行

**Prompt:** I have a stable job but no growth. Should I resign and move into a field where I have no formal experience?

**Expected shape:** Separate dissatisfaction from evidence, compare staying versus leaving, identify runway and obligations, propose a reversible test such as interviews or a small paid project, and set a decision date.

## 2. Three job offers / 三个工作机会

**Prompt:** Compare three offers when salary, commute, learning, manager quality, and family time point in different directions.

**Expected shape:** Build decision criteria, distinguish hard constraints from preferences, expose uncertain assumptions, and recommend targeted information gathering before applying weights.

## 3. Sunk-cost project / 沉没成本项目

**Prompt:** I have spent six months and substantial savings on a project with no traction. Should I continue?

**Expected shape:** Ignore past cost when assessing future investment, define the project’s critical assumption, design a time-boxed validation test, and specify continue/pivot/stop thresholds.

## 4. Sustainable study habit / 可持续学习习惯

**Prompt:** I repeatedly make ambitious study plans and stop after one week.

**Expected shape:** Diagnose friction and reward timing, shrink the starting behavior, define the environmental cue, track completion rather than duration first, and schedule a review.

## 5. Difficult conversation / 困难沟通

**Prompt:** A colleague repeatedly takes credit for my work. How do I address it without escalating too quickly?

**Expected shape:** Separate observable events from inferred intent, prepare a specific request, define boundaries, plan documentation and escalation criteria, and include fallback wording.

## 6. Moving cities / 是否换城市

**Prompt:** A new city offers better career prospects but would reduce family support and increase living costs.

**Expected shape:** Compare reversible and irreversible consequences, calculate runway, test the city before committing where possible, and identify a return option.

## 7. Large purchase / 大额消费

**Prompt:** I can afford a car, but buying it would reduce my emergency savings. How should I decide?

**Expected shape:** Clarify the job the purchase performs, include total ownership cost and opportunity cost, stress-test cash flow, and distinguish convenience from necessity.

## 8. Relationship boundary / 关系边界

**Prompt:** A family member frequently asks for money and becomes angry when I refuse.

**Expected shape:** Avoid diagnosing the person, define the user’s financial and emotional boundary, prepare consistent language, and plan for foreseeable reactions and safety concerns.

## 9. Learning direction / 学习方向

**Prompt:** I am choosing between learning coding, design, and sales, but I do not know which fits me.

**Expected shape:** Replace abstract self-judgment with small work samples, compare feedback speed and market access, and schedule parallel low-cost trials before committing.

## 10. Burnout-like overload / 长期过载

**Prompt:** I feel exhausted, cannot focus, and keep adding productivity systems.

**Expected shape:** Avoid medical diagnosis, check workload and recovery basics, reduce rather than add systems, propose a minimal stabilization plan, and identify signs that require professional support.
